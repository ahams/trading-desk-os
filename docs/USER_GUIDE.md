

# Introduction

## What is Trading Desk OS?

Trading Desk OS (TDOS) is an institutional-grade decision intelligence platform designed to help investors and traders evaluate securities from multiple independent perspectives and synthesize them into a single actionable decision framework.

Unlike traditional screeners that focus on a single dimension such as technicals or fundamentals, TDOS combines:

* Fundamentals
* Technical Analysis
* Liquidity & Volume Analysis
* Options Positioning
* Market Structure
* Game Theory
* Capital Structure Analysis (Merton Framework)
* Greenfield Growth Valuation
* Theme Leadership
* Macro Regime Analysis

The objective is simple:

> Transform information overload into actionable investment decisions.

---

# Understanding TDOS Output

TDOS separates:

1. Investment Quality
2. Trading Opportunity

These are not the same thing.

Example:

| Investment View | Trading View |
| --------------- | ------------ |
| Bullish         | Neutral      |

Meaning:

* Strong company
* Attractive long-term outlook
* Current entry may not be optimal

# Core Scores

## Fundamental Score

Measures:

* Revenue growth
* Earnings growth
* Margins
* Valuation
* Financial quality

Interpretation:

| Score | Interpretation |
| ----- | -------------- |
| 80+   | Excellent      |
| 60-80 | Good           |
| 40-60 | Average        |
| <40   | Weak           |

---

## Technical Score

Measures:

* Trend quality
* Entry quality
* Leadership

### Trend Quality

Evaluates:

* EMA alignment
* Trend persistence
* Relative strength
* Distance from highs

### Entry Quality

Evaluates:

* Pullback quality
* Breakout quality
* Risk/reward
* Volatility structure

### Leadership

Evaluates:

* Sector leadership
* Theme leadership
* Institutional sponsorship

---

## Liquidity Score

Evaluates:

* CMF
* Volume trends
* Accumulation
* Distribution

Purpose:

Determine whether institutional money is entering or exiting a stock.

---

## Options Score

Evaluates:

* Put/Call ratios
* Implied volatility
* Max pain
* Gamma positioning

Purpose:

Understand market positioning and option market expectations.

---

## Game Theory Score

Attempts to identify dominant participants:

* Institutions
* Dealers
* Momentum funds
* Short sellers
* Retail traders

Purpose:

Understand who currently controls price action.

# Specialized Engines

## Merton Capital Structure Engine

Measures:

* Distance to Default
* Probability of Default
* Debt Burden
* Financial Stability

Output:

Capital Structure Score

Interpretation:

Higher scores indicate stronger balance sheets and lower financial risk.

---

## Greenfield Growth Valuation Engine

Designed to evaluate businesses whose future value depends on capacity expansion, backlog growth, or future recurring revenue streams.

Examples:

* NeoCloud operators
* AI infrastructure
* Data centers
* Nuclear power
* Space infrastructure
* Platform businesses

Measures:

* ARR potential
* Capacity growth
* Backlog
* Unit economics
* Capital efficiency

---

# Trade Expectancy

Trade Expectancy measures the attractiveness of the current setup.

It is NOT a long-term expected return.

Example:

Trade Expectancy = -0.3%

Interpretation:

The current entry may not offer attractive risk/reward despite the company remaining fundamentally attractive.

# Decision Categories

## Strong Long

High conviction investment and trading alignment.

## Tactical Long

Bullish but requires disciplined execution.

## Constructive Watchlist

Good business with improving conditions.

## Watchlist Only

Mixed signals.

## Avoid

Unfavorable risk/reward profile.

# Interpreting a TDOS Report

Always read:

1. Investment View
2. Trading View
3. Key Reads
4. Bull Case
5. Bear Case
6. Action Plan

Example:

Investment View: Bullish

Trading View: Neutral

Action:

Accumulate on pullbacks while waiting for improved confirmation.
